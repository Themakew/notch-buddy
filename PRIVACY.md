# NotchBuddy Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

NotchBuddy is designed to respect your privacy.

## Data Collection

NotchBuddy does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

The app does not include analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party tracking SDKs.

## Local App Data

Any settings or preferences used by NotchBuddy are stored locally on your device. They are used only to provide the app experience and are not sent to the developer.

## Network Access

NotchBuddy does not require a user account and does not transmit personal data to a server controlled by the developer.

If a future version adds functionality that requires network access or data processing, this policy will be updated before or at the time that feature is released.

## Feature Requests and GitHub Issues

If you submit a feature request or comment through GitHub Issues, the information you provide is handled by GitHub according to GitHub's own terms and privacy policy.

Do not include sensitive personal information in public issues.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be published in this repository with a new "Last updated" date.

## Contact

For privacy questions or feature requests, please open an issue in this repository.
